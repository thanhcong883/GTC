# VietDesk — Customer Policy Handbook v3.1

*Internal reference document. This is the single source of truth for support agents.*

---

## 1. Plans & Pricing

| Plan | Price (USD/month) | Included seats | Ticket allowance / month |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $19 | 3 | 500 |
| Team | $59 | 10 | 3,000 |
| Business | $199 | Unlimited | 20,000 |

Annual billing is available on Team and Business only, at a 20% discount.
Exceeding the ticket allowance pauses new ticket intake until the next billing cycle. Overage cannot be purchased.

## 2. Refunds

- A **full refund** is available within **14 days** of the first payment on any plan.
- On annual billing, **no refund** is issued after **30 days**.
- **Business** customers only: unused whole months may be converted to account credit on cancellation. Starter and Team receive no pro-rated credit.
- Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.

## 3. First-response SLA

| Plan | Guaranteed first response |
|---|---|
| Starter | No SLA |
| Team | 8 business hours |
| Business | 2 hours, 24/7 |

Business hours mean Monday–Friday, 09:00–18:00 ICT.

## 4. Data residency & retention

- Data is stored in one of two regions, chosen at signup: **EU (Frankfurt)** or **Singapore**. The region cannot be changed after signup.
- Ticket retention: **90 days** on Starter and Team, **365 days** on Business.
- Attachments follow the same retention window as their parent ticket.

## 5. Support channels

VietDesk support is reachable by **email and in-app chat only**, Monday–Friday 09:00–18:00 ICT.

## 6. Escalation rule

If a customer question is not answered by this handbook, the agent must **not** guess. It must state that the topic is not covered by documented policy and route the ticket to a human.
