Day 7 · 30 Day Challenge

A playbook that ships a full ad pack

🖼️ See the batch
A PAM build · performance ad variations

One brief in. A brand-consistent ad pack out.

I picked one high-value format — performance ad variations — and turned it into a repeatable PAM Playbook: a SKILL.md of inputs → steps → checks → outputs. Then I ran it on two brands and scored every output. The honest result: the playbook nails the hard part (one identity across every size), but it doesn't yet emit the full placement set on its own.

01

The format, and why it fits PAM

Performance ad variations = one approved key visual + offer, adapted into every placement a paid campaign needs (Feed, Stories, Reels/TikTok, YouTube, display). It's the highest-frequency, most repeatable job in a marketing team — the same message, many sizes, every week. That's exactly what a Playbook is for, and it plays to PAM's two real strengths: brand-aware generation and recompose-to-ratio resizing (not dumb cropping). If any format was going to prove "reliably repeatable", this was the one.

Why a Playbook and not just a prompt. A playbook separates the method from the facts: the steps, safe-zone checks and stop conditions are written once, and each run only supplies this campaign's brand, message and CTA. That separation is what turns a good one-off prompt into output that's consistent instead of lucky.
02

The playbook — inputs → steps → outputs

In PAM a Playbook is a SKILL.md (Header + when-to-use, required inputs, ordered steps, checks, stop conditions). Here is the exact recipe I wrote and enabled, distilled to its three moving parts:

Inputs (from the brief)
  • Brand: logo, palette, fonts, tone
  • Product / subject
  • Headline ≤ 6 words (+ optional subhead)
  • Primary CTA text
  • Optional offer / legal line
  • Placement set (default 5)
Steps (the method)
  • Validate inputs; if brand/CTA/message missing → STOP, ask
  • Compose the MASTER at 1:1 (4 concepts, pick strongest)
  • Lock the approved 1:1 as source of truth
  • Recompose (not crop) into each placement, keeping text in safe zones
  • Run the checks; regenerate any fail once
  • Name & shortlist the pack
Outputs
  • 1:1 1080² — Feed
  • 4:5 — Feed portrait
  • 9:16 — Stories / Reels / TikTok
  • 16:9 — YouTube / display
  • 1.91:1 — link / banner
  • All one identity, named, shortlisted
The checks that define "reliable"

Every output must pass all five — this is the rubric I scored the batch against:

Brand — logo + colours + fonts, same identity across sizes  ·  Message — headline matches brief, ≤ 6 words, spelled right, no gibberish  ·  Legibility — headline + CTA readable at 50% zoom, text inside safe margins  ·  Composition — product is focal; nothing important in the outer 8% edge  ·  Consistency — a viewer recognises all sizes as ONE campaign.
▸ Read the full SKILL.md (paste-ready) 
## When to use
Use when a brief needs ONE campaign message adapted into MULTIPLE ad placements/sizes
and brand consistency across sizes matters. NOT for a single one-off hero image.

## Required inputs (else STOP and ask)
- Brand: logo, palette, fonts, tone (via @brand / brand workspace)
- Product / subject being advertised
- Core message / headline (<= 6 words) + optional subhead
- Primary CTA text
- Optional offer/claim and any mandatory legal text
- Placement set (default = the 5 below) or a custom list

## Steps (in order)
1. Validate inputs. If brand, message or CTA is missing -> STOP, request it.
2. Compose the MASTER at 1:1 (product hero, headline top, CTA bottom, logo fixed
   corner). Generate 4 concepts; pick the strongest.
3. Lock the approved 1:1 as the single source of truth.
4. RECOMPOSE (not crop) the master into each placement so headline + CTA + logo
   stay legible and inside safe zones:
     1:1 1080x1080 | 4:5 1080x1350 | 9:16 1080x1920 | 16:9 1200x675 | 1.91:1 1200x628
5. Run the checks on every output. Regenerate any that fail (once).
6. Name each <campaign>-<placement> and shortlist to the Gallery.

## Checks (every output must pass all)
- Brand / Message / Legibility / Composition / Consistency (see rubric above)

## Stop conditions
- Missing brand / CTA / message -> STOP, ask.
- If >= 2 of the set still fail after one regen -> STOP: the MASTER is weak,
  fix the 1:1 anchor, not the resizes.

## Output
A pack of placement-ready ads (default 5), brand-consistent, named and shortlisted.
03

How it actually ran

You don't press a "run" button — you @mention the playbook with a brief and PAM loads its method before acting. Here's the real sequence, both runs behaved the same way:

1
@mention + brief

Named the playbook and supplied only this campaign's facts (brand, product, headline, CTA).

2
Ran "Load playbook" → asked the right question

PAM loaded the SKILL.md, then asked its required-input question ("which product photo?") exactly where the recipe says to. With none, it composed the master without one.

3
Composed the MASTER at 1:1

Approved brief → Creative Studio → a 1:1 master with brand, headline, offer badge and CTA.

⏸
Paused here — needed a manual "continue"

The agent stopped after the master. It did not auto-run the resize step; I had to tell it to execute step 4 and enumerate the five placements.

4
Recomposed into the placement sizes

From the locked master, PAM recomposed (not cropped) the pack — repositioning text and CTA per ratio.

First honest finding. The playbook reliably drives the method and asks for the right inputs, but it is not autonomous end-to-end: it hands you the master and waits. "Repeatable" here means repeatable-with-one-nudge, not one-click.

05

Reliability scorecard — measured, not claimed

Scored against the five checks, across both runs (15 outputs). What held, what didn't:

Reliable
Brand identity across sizes

Logo, palette, fonts and layout system held on 15/15 outputs, both brands. This is the playbook's hardest job — and its strongest result.

Reliable
Headline & CTA correctness

Primary headline and CTA were correct and legible on every output. The recompose kept them in-frame at all ratios.

Not reliable
Complete placement set

Run 1 delivered all 5 (+1 extra); run 2 dropped the 1.91:1. The set isn't emitted consistently without an explicit, enumerated resize instruction.

Not reliable
Autonomy

Both runs paused after the master and needed a manual "continue". Repeatable-with-one-nudge, not one-click.

Caveat
Small-print & micro-text

Big type is clean; tiny text isn't. AI disclaimer overlapped the CTA (run 1) and vehicle livery garbled (run 2).

Caveat
Exact pixel dimensions

Aspect ratios are correct, but PAM picks its own resolution (e.g. 4:5 = 1229×1536, not the spec 1080×1350). Fine for ratio-based placements; not pixel-exact.

Verdict, stated plainly. The playbook is reliable where it matters most — brand and message hold across every size, on both brands. It is not yet reliable at emitting the full set autonomously. Two runs is enough to show that pattern; it is not enough to quote a percentage, so I don't.
06

What I'd fix, and what I won't claim

Make the resize step non-optional

The SKILL.md should force step 4 to enumerate and emit all five ratios as one action, so a run can't stop at the master or drop a size.

Add a safe-zone check for micro-text

An explicit "no disclaimer/legal text inside the CTA safe zone" rule would catch the 4:5 / 9:16 overlap before shortlisting.

Keep brand text off vehicles / props

AI garbles small livery text. The playbook should place brand marks as clean overlays, not rely on the model to render them inside the scene.

Prove repeatability across more briefs

Two runs shows the pattern but not a rate. Three to five briefs across verticals would turn "reliable where it matters" into a number worth stating.
