Part 3Model economics: multiplier bảo vệ model không đồng đều

Request COGS được dựng từ giá input/output công khai, 10% vendor discount, 5% retry overhead và $0.001 mỗi model invocation. Typical workload = 8k input + 1.5k output; heavy = 30k + 4k. Base case chưa trừ prompt-cache hoặc Vendor Batch discount.

DO NOT CALL TOTAL MARGIN A FLOOR

Base inference COGS is conservative relative to cache/Batch upside. Total contribution margin is not necessarily a floor because duration, paid tools, storage, egress and support are still incomplete. Hai sai lệch đi ngược chiều và phải được model riêng.

Part 10Migration, customer protection và kiểm chứng

  1. 0–30 ngày
    Instrument actual cost

    Log tokens, cache, model, tools, retries, branch path, worker count và 300+ credit workflows.

  2. 31–60 ngày
    Shadow-price normalized units

    So sánh old credit, proposed unit và vendor cost; đo bill shock theo customer cohort.

  3. 61–90 ngày
    Launch for new accounts

    Grandfather khách hiện tại, migration calculator, estimate range và workspace budgets.

MIGRATION DECISION

Light cohorts can pay less; heavy cohorts may pay 1.5–2.5×. Grandfathering should therefore be cohort-based: 6-month bill cap, side-by-side statement and automatic recommendation of the cheapest proposed plan.

Assumptions embedded in this analysis

  • Prepaid price: $9/1,000 credits.
  • Vendor discount: 10%.
  • Retry overhead: 5% of LLM cost.
  • Infra reserve: $0.001/model invocation.
  • Payment processing: 3%.
  • Base utilization: 40%.
  • Typical workload: 8k input / 1.5k output.
  • Heavy workload: 30k input / 4k output.
  • Base case excludes cache and Batch discounts.

What must be validated internally

  • Actual token distribution by model and plan.
  • Retry, cache and tool-call rates.
  • Customer-level margin cohorts.
  • Support hours and storage cost/account.
  • Distribution of 150, 300 and 300+ credit runs.
  • Bill shock under normalized units.
