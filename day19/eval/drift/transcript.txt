PROJECT KICKOFF — NORTHWIND LOGISTICS / RFP RESPONSE
Attendees: Mai Tran (Client, VP Operations), Duc Le (Client, Finance),
           Sarah Okonkwo (Vendor, Delivery Lead), Tom Rasmussen (Vendor, Solutions)
Recorded: 14 March, 10:02–10:41

Sarah opened by walking through the proposed three-phase rollout: discovery in
April, a pilot at the Hai Phong depot in May, and a decision gate before any
wider deployment. Mai confirmed the Hai Phong site is available for the pilot
and that her operations team can allocate two coordinators part-time.

Tom asked about the integration surface. Mai said the warehouse management
system is a 2019 on-premise install and that any connector would have to run
inside their network. She was clear this is a hard requirement, not a
preference — nothing that moves inventory data outside the perimeter will pass
their security review.

On commercials, Duc said the finance committee has not met on this yet. He said
we may consider a budget of up to $50,000 for the initial phase, but stressed
that nothing is signed off until the committee reviews the pilot results in
June. Sarah acknowledged that and said the proposal would be structured so the
pilot can be scoped independently of the wider rollout.

Mai raised a concern about the timeline. The depot runs a seasonal peak from
mid-May through June, and she does not want the pilot competing with peak
staffing. Sarah agreed to move the pilot window earlier, to the first two weeks
of May, and to confirm that in writing.

Duc asked whether training was included. Tom said onboarding for up to fifteen
users is included in the base scope and that anything beyond that would be
quoted separately.

Action items: Sarah to send a revised schedule with the earlier pilot window by
Friday. Tom to document the on-premise connector approach. Duc to bring the
proposal to the finance committee after the pilot review in June. Mai to confirm
the two coordinators by end of next week.
